Hi, I'm Branden Bean Drake. Like you, I contain multitudes.

At the heart of everything, I love people. The world is vast, beautiful, and interesting, and I certainly think it would be compelling enough to experience it all alone, but being able to share that experience with others exponentially multiplies its depth and richness. There are so many different ways to perceive the same phenomenon, and it is a delight to be able to trade perspectives, to inspire and be inspired by others, to collaborate in communities that celebrate both similarities and differences. I see a great many ways that we can yet make the world a better place for those living in it, and I want to do my part to facilitate joy, curiosity, and well-being in service of those I share this planet with. Few things motivate me like the chance to make someone's day better.

I'm also a born tinkerer. I love unraveling systems of all shapes and sizes, discovering all the ins and outs of how they function. I love the detective work of root cause analysis, starting with an unexpected behavior, following each telltale thread of causality further down the rabbit hole until I've finally identified the source and can tell the story of how everything connects. I love the challenge of making my own systems with processes and code, weighing the pros and cons of different design decisions, learning new techniques to grow my capabilities in service of a goal, and the simple joy that comes with having made something that works. I love solving puzzles, whether for practical purposes or just for the fun of it.

I'm interested in so many things. In addition to technology, I love learning about communication, philosophy, psychology, games, accessibility, and statistics. I love a good story, whether fictional or true. I love friendly, in-depth discussions. I love continuing to discover new interests that I previously wouldn't have thought I'd like, and I love growing into new capabilities that I previously wouldn't have thought possible. I love knowing that I will never reach a point where I have nothing left to learn.

As I continue to grow and change, I imagine that it will be interesting to return to these posts and see both how I have changed and how I have remained the same. And I hope what I post here can be helpful or interesting to you.

Thanks for dropping by!

-Branden
